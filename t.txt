PADDLEPRO ADVANCED FEATURES SPECIFICATION

1. DYNAMIC PRICING SYSTEM
------------------------
1.1 Time-based Pricing
- Peak Hours (Weekdays 17:00-22:00): Base price + 30%
- Off-peak Hours (Weekdays 06:00-16:00): Base price - 20%
- Weekend Rates: Base price + 25%
- Holiday Rates: Base price + 40%
- Early Morning (06:00-08:00): Base price - 30%

1.2 Demand-based Rates
- High Demand (>80% occupancy): Additional 20% surge
- Low Demand (<30% occupancy): 25% discount
- Historical booking patterns analysis
- Seasonal adjustments
- Special event impact consideration

1.3 Package Deals
- 10-Session Package: 15% discount
- Monthly Unlimited: Fixed rate with priority booking
- Group Training Package: 20% off for groups >4
- Tournament Package: Includes court time + amenities
- Corporate Packages: Customized rates for companies

1.4 Membership Tiers
Bronze:
- Basic court access
- Standard rates
- 24h advance booking

Silver:
- 10% discount on court bookings
- 48h advance booking
- Free equipment rental

Gold:
- 20% discount on all bookings
- 72h advance booking
- Priority court selection
- Free guest passes

Platinum:
- 30% discount on all services
- 7-day advance booking
- Dedicate

1.5 Special Event Pricing
- Tournament Rates: Custom packages based on duration and court count
- Training Camp Pricing: Bulk booking discounts
- Exhibition Matches: Premium pricing + spectator arrangements
- Private Events: Custom quotes based on requirements
- Seasonal Promotions: Holiday specials and seasonal discounts
